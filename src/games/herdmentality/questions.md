# 🐄 Herd Mentality Game Pack

## 🎯 Objective

Think like the group.

- Everyone answers the same prompt.
- The goal is **not to be unique**, but to match the **most popular answer**.
- The most common answer = points 🟢  
- A unique answer = you get the 🐄 **Pink Cow (bad!)**

---

## 🧠 How to Play

### 1. Setup
- 3–10 players (best: 4–8)
- Paper / phones to write answers
- Optional: score tracker

---

### 2. Game Loop

1. One player reads a question.
2. Everyone secretly writes their answer.
3. Reveal all answers **at the same time**.
4. Count matching answers:
   - Most common answer → players get **1 point**
   - Unique answer → gets the **Pink Cow 🐄**
5. Next player reads the next question.

---

### 🐄 Pink Cow Rule
- If you are the **only person** with your answer → you get the Pink Cow
- You **cannot win** while holding it
- You must lose it by:
  - Someone else becoming the new unique answer

---

### 🏆 Winning
- First player to reach **X points (e.g. 10)**  
- AND **not holding the Pink Cow**

---

## 💡 Strategy

- Don’t be clever — be **predictable**
- Think: *“What would MOST people say?”*
- Safe answers > creative answers
